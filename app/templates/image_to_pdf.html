How do I convert a JPG to PDF?

Click "Choose Images", select your JPG file, then click "Convert to PDF". Your JPG will be converted to a single-page PDF document, ready to download instantly.

Can I merge multiple images into one PDF?

Yes. Select multiple images (JPG, PNG, WebP, or any supported format), and our tool will combine them all into a single multi-page PDF. Each image becomes one page in the order you selected them.

What image formats can be converted to PDF?

We support JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, TIF, BMP, GIF, HEIC, and HEIF. This covers virtually every common image format, including iPhone photos.

Is the PDF quality good?

Yes. Images are embedded in the PDF at 100 DPI resolution, preserving their visual quality. The output is suitable for viewing, sharing, printing, and archiving.

Is there a file size limit?

Each individual image file can be up to 50 MB. You can upload as many images as you need. Very large images may take a few seconds to process.

How long are my files kept on the server?

Files are kept temporarily during processing and for a short period after (up to 60 minutes) to allow download. They are then automatically and permanently deleted from our servers.

Can I convert iPhone HEIC photos to PDF?

Yes. HEIC and HEIF — the default photo formats on iPhones and iPads — are fully supported. Simply select your HEIC files and convert them to PDF just like any other image format.

Do I need to install any software?

No software installation needed. Our image to PDF converter works entirely in your web browser on any device — desktop, laptop, tablet, or smartphone. Works on Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, and Android.
